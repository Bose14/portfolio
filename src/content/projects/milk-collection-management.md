## Overview

A software solution for dairy and milk collection operations.

## Scope

The system is designed around these areas of the operation:

- Milk collection records
- Morning and evening collection management
- Route and team management
- BMC and CC tracking
- Milk type tracking
- Tanker registration, tanker cells and capacity management
- Vehicle entry and dispatch tracking
- Operational reports
